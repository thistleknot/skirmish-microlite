Microlite Tactics

Tactical rules

Claim the altar for one action, then hold it through an enemy phase with no adjacent living enemy; leaving or falling loses the claim. Eliminating all enemies also wins.

Plain costs 1 movement; rubble costs 2; brush costs 2, or 1 for rangers. Shrine: one use, one action, up to 4 HP; spell debt stays unavailable.

Melee coordination: another attacker from a different side gains +1, or +2 opposite the first attack. Use the highest rear, Aid or coordination bonus; Sneak damage suppresses coordination. History resets each side's phase.

Hexes: pit/channel = -1 · table or barrel = +1 (Break table: Physical + STR, DC 10) · raised ledge = +2 (Physical Climb, DC 20) · ! = revealed trap; sprung pit traps remain open as depressions. Hatched = rock · solid/dashed door = closed/open · dark brown = furniture · arrow = facing · blue = move range · red = target · stairs icon = stairs down; up-stairs icon = way out.
